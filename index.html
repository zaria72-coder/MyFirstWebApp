Power BI

Power BI provides a business intelligence platform to organisations to turn data into reports, supporting data driven decisions.

Learn more on Power BI

Benefits of Power BI

  • Provides organisations with a report comprising data-driven insights, enhancing performance
  • Incorporates with other tools such as Excel, SQL, Azure, Microsoft Fabric and SharePoint.
  • Enhances data security and governance with its role-based access control and row-level security.

Why Power BI interests me?

Power BI interests me because I enjoy developing visualisations and dashboards to support my organisation in making data-driven decisions.
